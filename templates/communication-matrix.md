## Power / Interest Guide
| | Low interest | High interest |
|---|---|---|
| **High power** | Keep satisfied: concise updates, involve in key decisions | Manage closely: regular 1:1s, early warning of issues |
| **Low power** | Monitor: occasional general updates | Keep informed: regular updates, invite feedback |
