# Stakeholder Update Email — Template & Example

## Template
**Subject:** [Project] update: [one-line headline]

Hi all,

**Headline:** [The single most important message.]

**What's happened:**
- [Outcome 1]
- [Outcome 2]

**What's next:**
- [Next step, with date]

**What we need from you:**
- [Specific ask, with deadline, or "Nothing this week"]

Questions? Reply to this email or join the [channel / drop-in session].

Thanks,
[Name]

---

## Example
**Subject:** Insights platform update: Wave 1 live at all 15 sites

Hi all,

**Headline:** All 15 Wave 1 restaurants are now live on the insights platform, on schedule.

**What's happened:**
- Daily sales and covers data is now available by 9am for Wave 1 sites.
- Data matched the POS end-of-day reports at 99.7% during the first week.

**What's next:**
- Wave 2 (20 sites) starts Monday 3 March. Area managers have their site schedule.

**What we need from you:**
- Site GMs in Wave 2: please complete your 1-hour training by Friday 28 February.

Questions? Reply to this email or join the Teams drop-in on Thursday at 3pm.

Thanks,
Hassan
